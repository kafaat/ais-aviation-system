# Saudi personal data incident procedure

Use the operator's restricted incident system. The incident owner records the fields below and the controller assigns a named decision maker and alternate before go-live. Keep passenger names, passports, medical SSRs, credentials, full provider payloads and personal contact details out of GitHub, public support tickets and chat.

## Intake and decision

1. Open a case with its ID, reporter, incident start if known, discovery time and **time the controller became aware**, including timezone and evidence reference. Record whom the operator contacted and when. Contain the incident and preserve restricted evidence.
2. Record data types, approximate numbers of affected people, countries, affected processing activities, likely harm and effects on rights or interests. Record the decision maker, rationale and time. If the consequences are unknown, escalate for assessment immediately and retain the original awareness time.
3. If the condition in article 24(1) is met, the controller must notify the competent authority within 72 elapsed hours from awareness. Set an actual deadline and assign the submitter and alternate. A Friday awareness at 18:00 Riyadh gives a Monday deadline at 18:00 Riyadh. Preserve both instants. If required information is unavailable in time, submit available details and supply the outstanding details as soon as possible, with reasons for delay.
4. Use the Authority's Personal Data Breach Notification service on the National Data Governance Platform. Verify the operator's valid registration and authorized login in advance. Record the authority receipt/reference and submission time privately. A drafted form or internal ticket does not establish delivery.
5. Assess article 24(5) separately. Where harm to data or conflict with rights or interests is possible, notify affected individuals without undue delay in clear language. Explain the event, likely risks, mitigation, controller/DPO contact and protective actions. Record language, channel and delivery evidence. Track any independent reporting duties separately.
6. Preserve the submitted report, subsequent updates, mitigation actions and supporting evidence in the authorized incident record. Assign remediation, verify it and record closure approval.

## Report fields for the controlled record

| Section | Information to capture |
|---|---|
| Identification | Internal case, controller and DPO or other informed official, restricted contact, relevant provider and operator |
| Chronology | Start or best estimate, circumstances, awareness, decision, deadline, submission, subsequent updates; timezone on each timestamp |
| Scope | Categories and sensitivity, estimated/confirmed affected count, affected systems, data subjects and locations |
| Impact and response | Actual/potential risks, containment, corrective measures already taken, recurrence prevention |
| Data subjects | Notification decision, rationale, status, time, clear notice and evidence |
| Follow-up | Unavailable information and justification, named owner, due time, authority reference, report copy and evidence location |

## Transfer and retention review before enabling providers

Verify each deployment's configured regions, support access, subprocessors, contracts and actual fields sent. Provider names alone cannot establish a cross-border transfer. Start with Stripe (checkout), Railway (hosting/backups), Sentry (telemetry), Resend (email), Twilio (messaging) and Manus (chat). For each active flow record purpose, minimum fields, source and destination, processors, safeguards or other transfer basis, applicable risk assessment, erasure/return terms, owner, review date and approval evidence. For an unused adapter record that it is disabled and how this was verified.

Set retention per purpose and data class, including medical SSRs, passenger documents, manifests, financial records and backups. Record legal holds, access, expiry, deletion verification and the decision owner. Keep the existing retention-review guard active until the operator approves and tests an actual schedule.

Authority: [Saudi Data & AI Authority, PDPL Implementing Regulation, article 24](https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/SDAIA/about/Documents/ImplementingRegulation.pdf).
